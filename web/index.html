Force through the trap

What am I looking at?

Each panel moves the sphere along one axis through the trap (x and y at the axial equilibrium height zeq; z along the beam axis) and plots the optical force component along that axis. A stable trap needs the force to point back towards the equilibrium on every axis: the curve crosses zero with a negative slope. The slope there is the spring constant, and the area under the restoring part is the well depth. The beam propagates towards +z, so radiation pressure pushes the equilibrium slightly downstream of the focus.

F = Q · nm P / c    κ = −∂F/∂x |eq    Ω = √(κ/m)    U = −∫ F dx

Force map over beam intensity (xz plane)

What am I looking at?

The colour is the intensity of the trapping light in the plane containing the beam axis; arrows show the direction of the total optical force on the sphere if its centre sat at that point (arrow length is normalised, so only the direction is shown). In a bright tweezer the arrows converge just behind the focus; in a dark (bottle-beam) trap they converge on the intensity minimum, because a resonant particle can be pushed out of the light.

I = 4 k² |E|² · P / Pbeam (OTT field units)    F(r) from the full T-matrix at each point

Multipole contributions at zeq

What am I looking at?

The light scattered by the sphere is a sum of multipoles: electric/magnetic dipole (ED, MD), quadrupole (EQ, MQ), octupole (EO, MO)… Bars show the fraction of the beam power each one scatters with the particle at the trap position, and the textbook plane-wave Mie efficiencies of the same sphere. Switch multipoles off in “Teaching toggles” to see which ones make the trap work.

Psca/P = Σn,m 4(|pnm|² + |qnm|²)/Σ(|anm|² + |bnm|²)    Qsca = (2/x²) Σ (2n+1)(|an|² + |bn|²)

Parameter sweep

What am I looking at?

Every point is a full calculation (axial scan, equilibrium, stiffness, depth) with one parameter changed and everything else as in the panel on the left. Grey bands mark values where no 3D-stable trap exists (no restoring zero crossing along z, or a non-restoring lateral stiffness). Wavelength sweeps keep the particle index fixed unless the material preset tabulates it at that wavelength.

Γ/Ω = ω Psca / (5 m c² Ω²)    f = Ω / 2π
